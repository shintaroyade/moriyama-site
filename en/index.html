About

Hi, I'm
Shintaro Moriyama.

Born in Hyogo, Japan, I'm a first-year student at the College of International Management, Ritsumeikan Asia Pacific University (APU). I coach high school and university students in presentations, and I organize NEXT SPARK JAPAN, a presentation event where students talk about their own challenges.

Why presentations?

It all started on three stages.

  1. 2024
    Speak, and win.

    With my teammates, I won both the Grand Prix and the audience-voted Wow Award at Quest Cup, a national inquiry-based learning competition.

  2. 2025
    Fall, and speak again.

    I was eliminated once at the Tokai Student AWARD, but came back through the audience-voted wildcard and stood on the final stage.

  3. 2026
    Now, build the stage.

    I launched NEXT SPARK JAPAN, a presentation event that gives students a place to share their challenges.

I learned firsthand that the way you speak can move people's hearts — and change the outcome.

In an age when anyone can produce a decent idea with AI, what makes the difference is the ability to explain, in your own words, why you are the one doing it.

So rather than solving problems myself, I chose to back the people who take them on. I want people who have something to say — but can't yet put it into words — to step forward with confidence. That's why I study presentations, teach them, and create stages for them.
